Access your medical records

Use Fasten Connect and TEFCA to find and retrieve records from healthcare providers you have previously visited.

  1. Click Export Records and enter your personal email address.
  2. Check your email for a six-digit verification code from Fasten.
  3. Click Verify with CLEAR and complete the identity-verification steps.
  4. After returning, allow about two minutes while Fasten searches the TEFCA network for your providers.
  5. Select or sign in to a provider to consent to sharing those records.
  6. Click Continue and Close.
  7. On success, you can view and download your records in FHIR format.
i

Demo limitations

  • This demo is essentially stateless and your data is only cached for 24 hours.
  • Refreshing the page will require re-authentication with your provider.
  • You can only export one provider at a time; repeat the process for additional providers.
  • Exports can take 5+ minutes if you have a long medical history.
  • Some sources may not be working correctly.
  • Exports larger than 10MB may fail or slow down your browser. Contact us if you need help retrieving a large payload.

These limitations only exist in this stateless demo environment. Your actual integration will not have these limitations.

Need help or found a missing provider? Let us know so we can investigate.
